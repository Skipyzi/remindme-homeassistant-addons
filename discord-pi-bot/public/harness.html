Why the Home Assistant app

On an iPhone it can do what a web app can't: buttons to answer a task, confirm an action or snooze a reminder, a reply field, the cat as a picture, and a running task on the Lock Screen as a Live Activity. While it's on, notifications come only this way, so none arrive twice.

Where you are

Home Assistant has no person yet. Add one under Settings, People there, with your phone as its tracker.

In the Home Assistant app: Settings, Companion app, Location, turn on the location updates. In the iPhone's Settings, Home Assistant: Location "Always" with Precise Location, and Background App Refresh. Low Power Mode pauses it.

How it works

For places near you and reminders like "when I get home". Home Assistant's person for you says where your phone is: at home, in one of its zones, or anywhere on the map. Each message tells the assistant only roughly (home, a zone, or away and how far); for an address it asks your phone, which sends a fresh location when the last one is old.
