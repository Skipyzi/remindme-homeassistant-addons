Through the Home Assistant app

On an iPhone, the Home Assistant app can do what a web app can't: buttons to answer a job, confirm an action or snooze a reminder, a reply field, the thread's cat as a picture, and a running job on the Lock Screen as a Live Activity. Where you are

So it knows where you are when you ask, finds places near you, and can remind you "when I get home" or "when I leave the office". Home Assistant's person for you says where your phone is: at home, in one of its zones, or anywhere on the map. Each message tells the assistant only roughly (home, a zone, or away and how far); for an address it asks your phone, which sends a fresh location when the last one is old.

Home Assistant has no person yet. Add one under Settings → People there, with your phone as its tracker.

Your phone hasn't reported for a while. In the Home Assistant app on it: Settings → Companion app → Location, turn on the location updates; in the iPhone's Settings → Home Assistant, allow Location "Always" with Precise Location, and Background App Refresh. Low Power Mode pauses it.

Parcels

Add DHL with a free key of your own. It tracks every DHL parcel, Express and eCommerce shipment straight from DHL.

  1. Sign up at developer.dhl.com. It's free.
  2. Under My Apps, tap Create App, name it RemindMe and pick the API "Shipment Tracking - Unified".
  3. Open the app, show its API Key (not the secret) and paste it here.

DHL allows 250 checks a day for free. RemindMe checks each parcel every 45 minutes outside quiet hours (every 15 once it's out for delivery) and keeps 50 for when you ask. Other carriers (Hermes, DPD, GLS, UPS) still go through a TrackingMore key in the app's options.

News

A short digest of the day's news from Germany, the EU and the world, from the sources you tick. RemindMe reads their headlines, merges a story that several of them tell, and writes a line or two on each, with a link to every source. Your feeds

The agencies themselves (dpa, Reuters, AP, AFP) sell their wires to newsrooms and publish no feeds; dpa's comes through ZEIT ONLINE's newsticker. Any other source with an RSS feed can be added here, or under Connectors, where it can also tell you about new posts.

Price watches

Products RemindMe checks every few hours, on Geizhals (which compares German and Austrian shops) or on a shop's own page. You hear about it when the price falls under your target or, without one, drops by 5 % or more. Never during quiet hours.
